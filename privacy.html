Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vengeful Creations processes information when a user connects a social media account and uses the application to review and publish short-form videos.

1. Information we process

Depending on the platform and permissions granted by the user, the application may process:

  • basic account information returned by an official OAuth or platform API, such as an account identifier or display name;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection;
  • video files, titles, captions, publishing preferences and platform-generated post identifiers;
  • basic publishing status and performance information when the connected platform makes it available.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the application's requested functionality, including authenticating the connected account, presenting supported publishing options, uploading content after user authorization, checking publication status and displaying available performance information.

3. TikTok data

When a user connects TikTok, Vengeful Creations uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. TikTok access is limited to the scopes approved by the user. A video is posted only after the user has reviewed the available publishing settings and explicitly confirmed the post.

4. Storage and retention

Authentication tokens and operational publishing data may be stored by the application so that the authorized connection can continue between sessions. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, troubleshoot the service, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to the social platforms selected by the user when necessary to authenticate an account, upload a video, publish content or retrieve requested account or post information. We do not disclose account data to advertisers.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect credentials and application data. Users should not share access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or other authentication credentials with third parties.

7. User choices and deletion

Users may revoke the application's access through the relevant social platform's account settings. Revoking access prevents future authenticated requests. Users may also request deletion of locally stored account connection data by contacting the application operator.

8. Children's privacy

Vengeful Creations is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its integrations or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, account-data requests or deletion requests, contact the Vengeful Creations application operator through the contact address listed in the associated TikTok developer application.
